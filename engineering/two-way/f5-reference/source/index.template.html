REPOSITORY GEOMETRY / F5

The original frame, not a stand-in

A uses one 3U frame per tray. B uses two independent 3U frames with a 1 mm assembly gap. C retains the full 7U side board and its 3U + 3U + 1U padders. No rail is scaled; no side board is shortened.

Per tray

The 8 mm side spacers and 1 mm outer washers are retained. Fasteners, guard covers and tray bores remain a mounting study, not drilling instructions.

CLOSURE JOINT

Seating, location, retention

The spacer bears on reinforced tray rims. Its overlapping shoes locate against the outside and inside edges; the bands hold those seats engaged. The original case-to-case hinge is still absent.

Rigid contact carries compression

Metal seats transfer force near the enclosure perimeter. The thin gasket is for rattle control, not the only lateral restraint. Fixed internal reinforcement backs up the seats and strap stations.

Captured straps retain the stack

Two independent, non-elastic loop concepts pass through low-profile guides. Generic buckle covers and retained tails keep hard parts from snagging. Strap, buckle, stitching and support strength are still unspecified.

The playing surface stays clear

Tall locating skirts leave with the spacer. Fixed playing rims remain 2 mm high. The upper tray goes on the small independent 45° stand, not onto a hinge or a long external stay.

MANUAL HANDLING

Opening is still a supported lift

Switch off. Put the closed stack on a stable table, open the buckles and withdraw the free webbing ends from the upper tray guides, then support the shallow tray with both hands while lifting it clear of the locating shoes. Place it safely before opening the spacer sideways at its seams. Assemble the stand and transfer the shallow tray onto it; place the spacer and strap bundles in the empty trunk.

Real lead lengths and a collision-free turnover have not been established. A lead must never support a tray. The pictures are independent placements, not an opening animation.

NEXT PHYSICAL SAMPLE

A joint coupon, not the whole case

Try one representative rim/seat section, one guide and one actual strap on scrap material. Check hand access, fit, edge safety and resistance to sideways movement. Then use dummy loads representing each populated tray to evaluate retention in the intended handling orientations, with the assembly restrained against falling.

There is no safe working load, drop rating or passing physical test in this package. A suitable trunk does not compensate for loose rails, a buckling spacer or a closure that slips.

Scope & sources

F5 is a dimensional workbench, not production CAD. The rail meshes are the complete pinned source meshes, with rigid placement only. PCB outer vertices, padder rectangles and mounting datums follow the source; rounded hole/slot contours are display approximations. Use the original KiCad files for fabrication, not these viewer meshes. Shapes contain overlapping assembled-part envelopes and representative fasteners, not manufacturing bores. No strength, drop, tip, temperature, electrical-safety or fixed-length cable simulation is claimed. A source/assumption register and physical test record are included in the ZIP.

Befaco's belt-retained empty cover is a closure precedent, not validation of a populated upper tray. The stand approach references zudo-stand, not its dimensions or load rating. Tendrils' 12 mm plugged height informs the low-profile study. Frame provenance and comparison results are in RAIL-FRAME-INTEGRATION.md. Earlier transport research is retained in the F4 archive notes.
