01 / KEEP THE INTERIOR

Thicker panels grow outward.

The cavity stays 226.4 × 331 × 89.5mm. The floor increases from 1.5 to 5mm, so the rim and existing frame datums rise together by 3.5mm. No scaled rails. The original six rail profiles and eight PCB shapes are now included.

02 / ACTUAL BED FIT

Long sides remain single pieces.

341mm side walls print flat at 45°. Only the broad floor and lid top are divided. Both blue joining strips are removed. Equal floor halves are fastened independently to the perimeter.

A stepped plate, not a thin frame.

A 5mm outer wall, inward locator, and segmented shelf follow the uploaded STL idea. Stock L brackets hold the roof. The lid lifts straight off; transport still needs external straps.

Print inventory, reference interpretation, and verification scope

Prototype, not production approval. The 12 structural print parts and optional washer files are unchanged from P2; their hashes are verified. P3 adds 66 existing/reference rail-unit components to the preview only. Rails, PCB parts, spacers and their hardware are not added to the case print list. Lid detail: the uploaded STL measures approximately 317.408 × 48.500 × 10mm, treating unitless STL coordinates as millimetres. The adapted lid has a 38mm rise to its roof underside, 2mm more than that reference's inferred 36mm, to clear the modeled M5 roof-fastener tips. Locator overlap is 7.5mm and nominal side clearance is 0.3mm. Local support is needed under the offset locator in the proposed flat print orientation.

Printer constraint: every generated print has a native edge below 350mm and fits a 325 × 320mm rectangle with a 5mm XY brim allowance. This is a CAD-envelope check, not a Bambu Studio slice, nozzle-access proof or warpage test. Use the actual H2D single-nozzle profile; a dual-nozzle 300 × 320mm profile is different.

P2 seam change: floor and roof joining strips, their eight fastening stacks, and eight dedicated panel holes are removed. Floor halves are identical after a 180° rotation. New bracket axes at ±25mm are a preview design choice, not a measured user dimension. Both sets of halves are held at the perimeter, with no direct middle-seam connection. Flex and strength remain untested.

Mechanical scope: nominal printed-part/bracket intersections, sampled lid removal, split seams, STL/STEP read-back and envelopes are checked. P3 replaces the old envelope with the original six 204mm rail meshes and eight PCB solids. The rail source has duplicate/non-manifold faces; it is retained as a reference mesh, not certified manufacturing geometry. The added solid PCBs/spacers/hardware are checked against the unchanged case panels and bracket solids. Rail mesh extents are checked against the case walls; no full rail-mesh/fastener collision proof is claimed. The knob zone is an assumption, not a guarantee for every module. Real hardware, ASA creep, bed adhesion, tightening, electrical integration and transport loads remain untested.

Variant boundary: this study does not replace, commit or deploy the aluminum full-set plan. No supplier coupons or sample order is proposed. There are 20 body L brackets and 8 lid L brackets (28 total). The four new center-adjacent brackets replace the previous two central body brackets. Rail-mount holes remain unchanged.

Mount hardware: the side-mount grip is 15.6mm. M5×25 is shown as a provisional length for the 5mm wall, not a confirmed order item. Rail-end screws retain the source schematic M5×40 envelope. Neither threads nor actual purchased screw dimensions are qualified.
